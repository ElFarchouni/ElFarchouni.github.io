A detailed record of my academic background, research roles, and technical training in

## Research Experience

<div class="timeline" markdown>

<div class="timeline-entry" markdown>
<span class="date-pill">Oct 2023 – Present</span>

### PhD Researcher in Integrated Hydrology & Groundwater Recharge

**Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), International Water Research Institute (IWRI)**, Benguerir, Morocco
**University of Florence (UNIFI), Department of Agriculture, Food, Environment and Forestry (DAGRI)**, Florence, Italy

- Conducting research on groundwater recharge and conceptual modeling of the Haouz–Mejjate aquifer system (Atlas of Marrakech).
- Developing a coupled SWAT–MODFLOW framework to quantify recharge and evaluate the effects of land-use/land-cover change and climate variability on water resources.
- Integrating hydrochemistry, stable isotopes, geophysics, remote sensing, and machine learning to assess recharge dynamics in semi-arid regions.
- Multidisciplinary framework combining field campaigns, data analysis, and integrated hydrological modeling.

<div class="timeline-entry" markdown>

### Fieldwork & Data Collection

- **Geophysical surveys:** Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT), Magnetic Resonance Sounding (MRS), seismic surveys, and EM38.
- **Hydrogeological monitoring:** Piezometric data monitoring systems, streamflow measurements, and data analysis.
- **Water sampling:** Collection and analysis of isotopic (δ²H, δ¹⁸O) and hydrochemical samples.
- **Aquifer characterization:** Execution and interpretation of pumping tests.
- **Socio-economic surveys:** Design and implementation of farmer questionnaires on irrigation practices, groundwater recharge, and water management strategies.
